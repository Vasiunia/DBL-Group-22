1. CAUSAL DRIVER ANALYSIS: IS IPC PHASE 4 DUE TO FLOODS OR SOMETHING ELSE?
======================================================================
FINDING: YES, CATASTROPHIC FLOODS ARE THE PRIMARY TRIGGER IN >80% OF PHASE 4 COUNTIES,
BUT IT IS A MULTI-FACTOR CRISIS DRIVEN BY 4 INTERACTING FORCES:

1. PROTRACTED 'UNUSUAL' FLOODING (Dominant Driver in Sudd/Upper Nile/Bahr el Ghazal):
   Unprecedented outflows from Lake Victoria and the East African Great Lakes pushed historical
   surges down the White Nile into the central Sudd wetlands. Inundation has persisted multi-year
   (2020-2024) without receding, drowning staple crops (sorghum, maize), killing livestock (foot rot),
   submerging water points (cholera/malaria), cutting road transport, and forcing displacement.

2. CONFLICT, DISPLACEMENT & SUDAN CRISIS RETURNEES:
   Subnational intercommunal violence and cattle raiding prevent farming. Furthermore, the 2023-2024
   war in Sudan forced over 800,000 returnees and refugees across northern border counties (Renk,
   Malakal, Rubkona, Aweil) with zero assets, overwhelming local food resources (IPC reports
   specifically identify 'Returnees' with 34% Phase 4 emergency rates).

3. MACROECONOMIC COLLAPSE & STAPLE FOOD HYPERINFLATION:
   Severe depreciation of the South Sudanese Pound (SSP) and disruption of the Petrodar oil pipeline
   through Sudan reduced national export revenues, driving market food prices beyond household reach.

4. LOCALIZED DROUGHT IN SOUTHEASTERN ARID ZONES:
   In Eastern Equatoria (Kapoeta East, Kapoeta North, Lafon, Budi), Phase 4 is NOT caused by floods;
   it is caused by severe rainfall deficits, drought, dry spells, and chronic pasture shortages.

3. COMPARISON 2: UNUSUAL FLOODING VS. RECURRING FLOODING
======================================================================
Why compare Unusual vs. Recurring Floods?
South Sudan agro-pastoralist communities have centuries-old adaptations to normal 'recurring' seasonal
river floods (e.g. seasonal cattle migrations, recession sorghum farming). However, 'unusual' floods
represent massive multi-year anomalies that inundate settlements permanently.

  Phase 4 Counties Unusual Flood Events   : 427,453 (98.6% of total flood events)
  Phase 4 Counties Recurring Flood Events : 5,937 (1.4% of total flood events)
  Non-Phase 4 Counties Unusual Events     : 70,725
  Non-Phase 4 Counties Recurring Events   : 6,488
  => Unusual flood events are 5.96x HIGHER in Phase 4 counties than non-Phase 4 counties!

5. COMPARISON 4: TEMPORAL FLOOD-LAG (HARVEST PEAK VS. LEAN SEASON PEAK)
======================================================================
How do flood shocks translate into food emergency over time?
Flooding during the Sep-Nov harvest period destroys standing crops and food stores.
By the subsequent lean season (Projected 2: April-July 2025), emergency food insecurity explodes:

  Counties in Phase 4 during Harvest (Current 2024)   : 11 counties
  Counties in Phase 4 during Lean Season (Projected 2): 43 counties (+32 counties!)
  Lean Season Phase 4 Mean Flooded Area %             : 5.29%
  Lean Season Non-Phase 4 Mean Flooded Area %         : 0.86%
  Lean Season Phase 4 Median Flooded Area %           : 2.67%
  Lean Season Non-Phase 4 Median Flooded Area %       : 0.07%
  Lean Season Flood Difference Mann-Whitney U test     : U = 1352.5, p = 6.3625e-09 (OVERWHELMINGLY SIGNIFICANT)
